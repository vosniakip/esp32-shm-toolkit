# Referências metodológicas

1. INVENSENSE. *MPU-9250 Product Specification*. Document
   PS-MPU-9250A-01, Revision 1.0, 2014. Especifica faixas, sensibilidades,
   tolerâncias, ruído, filtros, taxa e características elétricas.
   <https://www.digikey.com/en/resources/datasheets/invensense/mpu-9250-product-spec>

2. IEEE. *IEEE Std 1293-2018: IEEE Standard Specification Format Guide and
   Test Procedure for Linear, Single-Axis, Nongyroscopic Accelerometers*.
   Inclui procedimentos de teste, análise multiposição, ruído, filtragem,
   vibração e choque para acelerômetros, inclusive micromecânicos.
   <https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/1293/5590/>

3. TEDALDI, D.; PRETTO, A.; MENEGATTI, E. A robust and easy to implement
   method for IMU calibration without external equipments. *2014 IEEE
   International Conference on Robotics and Automation*, p. 3042–3049, 2014.
   DOI: 10.1109/ICRA.2014.6907297.
   <https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA.2014.6907297>

4. FROSIO, I.; PEDERSINI, F.; BORGHESE, N. A. Autocalibration of MEMS
   accelerometers. *IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement*,
   v. 58, n. 6, p. 2034–2041, 2009. O método usa o módulo da gravidade em
   posições estáticas para estimar offset, escala e fatores cruzados.
   <https://expertise.unimi.it/resource/item/120540>

5. WELCH, P. D. The use of fast Fourier transform for the estimation of power
   spectra: a method based on time averaging over short, modified
   periodograms. *IEEE Transactions on Audio and Electroacoustics*, v. 15,
   n. 2, p. 70–73, 1967.
   <https://research.ibm.com/publications/the-use-of-fast-fourier-transform-for-the-estimation-of-power-spectra-a-method-based-on-time-averaging-over-short-modified-periodograms>

6. EL-SHEIMY, N.; HOU, H.; NIU, X. Analysis and modeling of inertial sensors
   using Allan variance. *IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and
   Measurement*, v. 57, n. 1, p. 140–149, 2008.
   DOI: 10.1109/TIM.2007.908635.
   <https://doi.org/10.1109/TIM.2007.908635>

7. ISO. *ISO 16063-21:2003: Methods for the calibration of vibration and shock
   transducers — Part 21: Vibration calibration by comparison to a reference
   transducer*. Base para a etapa posterior de comparação dinâmica.
   <https://www.iso.org/standard/27053.html>

8. ISO. *ISO 16063-31:2009: Methods for the calibration of vibration and shock
   transducers — Part 31: Testing of transverse vibration sensitivity*.
   <https://www.iso.org/standard/36407.html>
